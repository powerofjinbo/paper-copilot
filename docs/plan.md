# Plan: paper-copilot, a sentence-first, repo-grounded LaTeX writing platform

## Context

**The problem.** Jinbo writes HEP papers in English as a non-native speaker. AI drafts read better than their own English but carry a strong "AI flavor", do not sound like them, and sometimes invent numbers or drift in meaning. Existing tools (Overleaf AI Assist, Writefull, Paperpal, Jenni, Papers AI, the closing OpenAI Prism) act on selections or whole documents, never read the analysis code, have no personal voice layer, and give the author no sentence-level control over an AI draft.

**The vision (user's words, paraphrased).** Before writing, the AI reads the project repository and the literature and forms an outline. An Overleaf-like, LaTeX-first editor with two modes: (A) ghost text, the next sentence predicted in light gray, the human types or accepts, downstream predictions re-plan after every human sentence; (B) the AI drafts the whole section, then every period-delimited sentence is a unit the human accepts, edits, or instructs. The user's own `hep-paper-writing` skill must govern the AI's writing. Output must sound like the researcher and never invent numbers, claims, or citations.

1. Form factor: local-first web app on their laptop (Vite + React + CodeMirror 6; `.tex` source is the single source of truth; local TeX Live compile; PDF preview).
2. Manuscripts live on Overleaf with a paid/institutional project owner → Overleaf git bridge is the sync path.
3. First real document: the Canto manuscript (GNN tracker for displaced particles, first author, PRD submission in final polishing). ## Product (final design, condensed from the judged synthesis)

**One paragraph.** A local-first LaTeX editor (one command opens the browser) for a HEP researcher who writes English as a second language and wants AI help without AI flavor. Before any prose, the AI reads the analysis repository and the cited literature and builds a **fact store** and a **draft outline** that the author rewrites and approves. Writing happens one sentence at a time, in two modes over the same outline and fact store: **ghost mode** (next sentence in gray, grounded in facts and the author's voice, re-planned after every human sentence) and **draft-then-review mode** (the AI drafts a section; every sentence stays visibly "not yet yours" until the author accepts, edits, instructs or rejects it; downstream sentences re-plan after each decision). Every AI sentence is governed by the user's `hep-paper-writing` skill, conditioned on the author's corpus and edit history, grounded (every number, cut and `\cite` traces to an artifact locator or a resolved `.bib` key, or becomes a placeholder), logged per sentence so the venue disclosure writes itself, and never pushed to co-authors unreviewed. The sentence is the unit of thought, review, provenance and re-planning.

### Core loops

**Ghost mode (A).** Per-section setting shown in the Outline panel: `complete-my-sentence` (default for Abstract, Introduction, Conclusions: the AI continues only after the author typed 3+ English words), `auto-propose` (default for Methods, Results, captions: whole next sentence at a sentence boundary, the user's "gray pre-writing"), `questions-only` (no prose; the panel asks what the paragraph still owes). Ctrl+Space requests/regenerates anywhere.
1. Cursor enters an empty slot → no API call; the plan column shows the next move (e.g. `result: f.g1.delta_points`), the Outline panel shows unsaid claims (what to say, not how).
2. **Intent notes**: a line that is mostly CJK is an intent line (teal; excluded from segmentation, lint and compile via a shadow copy with blanked lines so SyncTeX line numbers hold; blocks push). Ctrl+Enter renders it into one English sentence in the author's voice, grounded in facts (state `ai-from-intent`); Alt+Enter gives 2–3 alternatives. The note moves to the sidecar, visible on hover.
3. Trigger: in `auto-propose`, a committed boundary (`.`/`?`/`!` outside the skill's `ABBR` set, next-char rule) + 800 ms, or a 500 ms pause after 3+ words; `complete-my-sentence`: pause only. Never per keystroke. Esc suppresses until the next boundary.
4. Request: stateless single shot to `claude-haiku-5-5`, `thinking: {type: 'disabled'}`, `output_config: {effort: 'low'}`, `max_tokens: 200`, JSON schema `{text, fact_ids[], cite_keys[], fulfilled_move_ids[]}` (text first). Cached blocks A/B/C (below); volatile tail = last two paragraphs, paragraph plan (`said`/`remaining`), sentence prefix, intent note, six retrieved author sentences for this move. Constraints: continue the prefix verbatim, ≤ 35 words, no number outside listed facts, `\cite` only from the allowed list, no new notation.
5. Gate (below, ~5 ms) runs on the whole sentence → **atomic paint** (no show-then-retract); one regeneration with the failure as feedback, then "no grounded suggestion".
6. Shown: one sentence at 45% gray; fact-backed numbers dotted-underlined; placeholders as orange chips. The AI's further plan never enters the document; next 2–3 moves appear faintly in the Outline panel.
7. Keys: Tab accept; Ctrl/Cmd+Right accept next word; Esc dismiss; Alt+]/Alt+[ cycle alternatives.
8. On accept: the sentence becomes a unit on its own line (semantic line breaks), state `ai-drafted`, facts/cites linked, model + prompt hash logged.
9. Re-planning, three levels: immediate (fact ids and cite keys mark claims `said`); by-product (`fulfilled_move_ids[]` advances the plan with no extra call); asynchronous (`claude-sonnet-5-5`, `between_tools`, effort low) at paragraph end or when `fulfilled_move_ids` is empty twice in a row (author went off-plan): returns 1–4 revised intents or declares the paragraph complete. The re-planner never edits text.

**Draft-then-review mode (B).**
1. Entry per paragraph or section: `Draft from my notes` (default when intent notes exist) or `Draft from outline`.
2. Drafter = Agent SDK session (`claude-opus-5-5`, skill + the one reference the skill's routing table names) with MCP tools `get_outline`, `get_plan`, `get_facts`, `get_sentences`, `get_lit`, `lookup_cite`, `propose_paragraph`. Per sentence `{text, move, fact_ids, cite_keys, placeholders, note_zh, referee_question?}`. PostToolUse hook runs the gate and returns failures as `updatedToolOutput`; after two retries a sentence lands as `unsupported` (red ring), never dropped. Paragraphs land atomically; `Continue drafting` resumes the session.
3. **Structure pass first** (the skill's top-down rule): drafted paragraphs appear as job cards to reorder/delete/rename before any sentence shows; then `Start sentence review`.
4. Presentation: sentences enter the `.tex` one per line in state `proposed` at 60% ink with a dotted gutter bar and a faint line stating the paragraph's job. Nothing is black until the author acts. Alt+Down/Up jumps between proposed sentences; status bar `12 of 37 reviewed`.
5. Actions: Ctrl+Enter accept; typing edits in place (`ai-edited`, gutter fill ∝ edit distance); Ctrl+I instruct (Chinese or English, e.g. "把基线放在前面"): one rewrite as a word-level diff with the drift checklist (actor, scope, direction, condition) and a note in the user's language, frequent instructions become one-click chips; Ctrl+G grammar-only fix (minimal diff, state unchanged, `grammar_fixed` flag; excluded from voice learning); Ctrl+. sentence card with single-letter actions: `l` alternatives (2–3 side by side), `r` rewrite myself (clears text, keeps intent and fact chips as a gray hint; result `human` with `ever_ai: true`), `x` reject (sentence becomes `[claim: <text>?]` so no claim is silently lost), `h` history.
6. Re-plan after an edit: 1.0 s after leaving an edited/instructed/rejected sentence, the re-planner reviews only downstream `proposed` sentences of the same paragraph → `keep | revise(text) | drop | insert_before(text)`; revised sentences get a `↻` chip with a diff; accepted sentences are never changed automatically (comment only). Section-wide re-plan only on an explicit button or when a claim is removed.

**Shared state.** Both modes read/write the same per-paragraph `Plan {job, claim_ids, said: {claim_id: s_id}, remaining: [{intent, fact_ids}]}`; both prompts render the fact table from the same code path so the modes cannot disagree about a number.

### Data model (sidecar, sibling of the Overleaf clone so nothing can leak to co-authors)

```
~/papers/canto/
  tex/                      Overleaf git clone (or ols folder): the only thing synced
  .paper/
    project.json outline.json facts.json claims.json figures.json terminology.json
    evidence.jsonl history.jsonl          append-only, git-friendly
    paper.db                              SQLite: sentences, versions, lint, jobs
    brief/repo_brief.md brief/lit_brief.md
    lit/index.json lit/<texkey>.json lit/src/<arxiv-id>.tar.gz
    voice/corpus.jsonl voice/stats.json voice/memo.md voice/edit_pairs.jsonl
    sync/base/<file>.tex                  last-synced snapshots (three-way base)
    build/                                latexmk output and shadow sources
    claude/.claude/skills/hep-paper-writing -> ~/.claude/skills/hep-paper-writing (symlink)
```

- **Sentence unit** (ULID): `{id, file, start, end, line, hash, ctx_hash(prev+next), text_norm, section_id, paragraph_id, move, state, ever_ai, model, model_version, prompt_hash, created_at, accepted_at, edit_distance, intent_note, facts[], cites[], claims[], placeholders[], lint[], alternatives[], comments[], orphaned, split_from}`. States: `human | ai-from-intent | ai-drafted | ai-edited | proposed | external | placeholder | unsupported`; flags `grammar_fixed`, `ever_ai` (never clearable; no "mark human-written" action). `hash` normalization: strip comments, collapse whitespace, math → `⟨M⟩`, `\cite{k}` → `⟨C:k⟩`, `\ref{l}` → `⟨R:l⟩`. Versions table `{s_id, version, ts, text, origin ∈ {human_typed, human_edit, ai_ghost, ai_intent, ai_draft, ai_rewrite, grammar_fix, replan, external_edit}, model, prompt_hash, instruction, lang, parent_version}`, mirrored to `history.jsonl`.
- **Outline**: `{story{text, key_phrase}, gap, enabler, ladder[{case, fact_ids}], claim_figures[{claim_id, figure_id}], headline{fact_id, baseline_fact_id, condition}, framings[], sections[{id, title, question, template, ghost_mode, paragraphs[{pid, job, moves[], claim_ids, intent_notes, plan}]}]}`; every item has `provenance: author | ai-proposed | ai-edited`. The five items are the skill's Step 0.
- **Fact** (HEP-Pilot shape, extended; real Canto example): `{"id":"f.g1.delta_points","kind":"number","value":2.319,"sigfigs":3,"unit":"points","uncertainties":{"total":0.279,"boot":0.164,"seeds":0.226},"definition":"Efficiency difference, Canto-L (ttbar) minus acorn (HNL)+LM, population P_G, generic_iso union test, n=8710","artifact":"results/addendum_g_g1_v1_claim.json","locator":"#/delta_points","artifact_sha256":"…","fingerprint":"sha256(sha||locator)","stale":false,"validation_state":"candidate","aliases":["2.3 points","2.32 points"]}`. `kind ∈ {number, cut, definition, sample, setting, figure, table}`; aliases derived from value + sigfigs only; `validation_state` defaults to `candidate` and rises only via an `evidence.jsonl` record `{fact_ids[], command, artifact, sha256, ts}`.
- **Claim map** (note-writer contract): `{claim_id, text, evidence[fact_id], citations[texkey], qualification, status: supported|placeholder|excluded, sentence_ids[], section, move}`. **Figures**: `{figure_id, path, sha256, producer_script, claim_ids, caption_s_ids, exists|needs_run}`. **Voice**: `corpus.jsonl {text, source, section_type, unassisted}`, `stats.json`, `edit_pairs.jsonl {s_id, ai_text, human_text, instruction, section_type, grammar_only}`, `memo.md`. **Lint**: `{s_id, check, source: style_check|kobak|heuristic, msg, word, corpus_rate, user_rate, dismissed}`.
- **Anchor survival.** Live: ranges map through every CodeMirror transaction (`ChangeSet.mapPos`); only the edited paragraph re-segments. External change (pull, `ols`, file watcher): three-way against `sync/base`: exact `hash` match re-anchors (`ctx_hash` tie-break); else best candidate within 8 lines with normalized similarity ≥ 0.6 and an agreeing neighbor → `external` with an `external_edit` version; one-to-two split keeps the id on the best match and links `split_from`; otherwise `orphaned` and listed (metadata never auto-deleted); new lines become `external` sentences. Import offers a reversible one-sentence-per-line normalization of prose paragraphs (with co-authors' consent).

### Grounding pipeline

All agents read-only: `tools: ['Read','Glob','Grep','Skill']`, `disallowedTools: ['Bash','Write','Edit','WebFetch','WebSearch']`, `permissionMode: 'dontAsk'`, a PreToolUse hook denying Write/Edit/Bash, `maxBudgetUsd` per run, `CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_AUTO_MEMORY=1`.
- **Deterministic extractor** (Node, no model): registers every number in `results/*.json`, `*_claim.json` and CSV summary rows as a fact with artifact path, JSON pointer or row/column locator, SHA-256 and fingerprint; definitions come from artifact metadata (`A`, `B`, `population`, `split`, `n_targets`, CSV header columns) so values cannot be mislabeled; `verify_fact` re-reads at the locator on every open and marks `stale` on fingerprint change, queuing linked sentences for the Consistency view. On Canto: `eff_A`, `eff_B`, `delta_points`, `sigma_points`…, CSV tables, cuts from `docs/definitions.md` (pT > 1 GeV, |η| < 4, ≥ 5 space points).
- **repo-reader** subagent (`claude-sonnet-5-5`, between_tools, medium effort, `additionalDirectories: [repoDir]`): reads README, `docs/`, `results/`, `configs/`, figure producers, decision logs → `repo_brief.md` (≤ 6k tokens: summary, pipeline stages in the repo's own names, samples/definitions, result tables with the artifact behind each cell, claims register from `docs/preregistration.md`, figure inventory `exists|needs_run`, terminology, unknowns) plus fact candidates **as locators only**; the backend reads the values. README-prose-only numbers become `candidate` facts flagged "prose-only". Scope = a glob list the user confirms.
- **lit-reader** (Sonnet) reaches the outside only through in-process MCP tools (`inspire_lookup` batched + token bucket 15/5 s + cache; `arxiv_src`; `s2_lookup`), resolves every `.bib` entry to an INSPIRE texkey, writes `lit/<texkey>.json {texkey, recid, arxiv, title, abstract, quotable:[{claim, quote, locator}], usable_for[]}` (the shape of the user's `docs/paper_facts.md`) for the 5–10 closest papers.
- **Citation validation**: a key must exist in the `.bib` and be `resolved` (INSPIRE/S2) or `user_confirmed`; prompts receive only such keys with one-line descriptions; the gate turns any other key into `[ref]`.
- **outliner** (`claude-opus-5-5`, skill + `story-and-structure.md`) runs only after the author fills the five-item form and proposes **three framings** as `ai-proposed` items.

### Skill and voice system

- **Loading**: skill stays the user's git checkout, symlinked under `.paper/claude/.claude/skills/`; agents run with `cwd: .paper/claude`, `settingSources: ['project']`, `skills: ['hep-paper-writing']`, `Skill` in `tools`; repo and `tex/` via `additionalDirectories`. Progressive disclosure: SKILL.md always; one or two references by the skill's routing table (outline/abstract/intro/conclusions → story-and-structure; section drafting → layering-and-flow; ghost and rewrites → sentences-and-words; captions/results → figures-and-evidence), each wrapped `<reference fictional_examples="true">`; `style_check.py` runs in the sidecar and never enters context. The direct-API ghost path reads the same SKILL.md from disk.
- **Prompt layout** (identical for Haiku/Sonnet/Opus; caches are per model): block A `[SKILL.md + reference + memo.md + fictional-examples rule]` with `cache_control: {type:'ephemeral', ttl:'1h'}` (~12k tokens); block B `[repo brief + lit brief + frozen outline (five items + section jobs) + terminology + allowed cite keys + compact fact table]` 5-min TTL (15–20k tokens), refreshed by use and a `max_tokens: 0` keep-alive every 4.5 min while focused; block C `[exemplars for this section type]` 5-min TTL; volatile tail after the last breakpoint. Model/thinking/effort constant per route. Pre-warm A+B on open. Phase 0 verifies whether a `max_tokens: 0` pre-warm may carry `output_config.format`; fallback: plain text + stop sequence + trailing `facts:` line parsed by the gate.
- **Voice, four sources at once** (few-shot alone is weak): (1) the author's unassisted corpus (FCC-ee and LDMX notes, thesis proposal, texts the author confirms; not TIME; Canto README not assumed unassisted); (2) measured norms of that corpus beside the skill's norms; (3) six nearest author sentences for the current move and section type (BM25 over normalized text); (4) a CIPHER-style memo (≤ 400 words, visible, editable) refreshed by Sonnet when 10 new edit pairs with edit distance > 10% accumulate; grammar-only pairs excluded.
- **Anti-anchoring**: author writes the five items before the AI proposes; three framings; the AI never writes the first words in voice-critical sections unless asked; alternatives on demand; Chinese intent rendered into the author's English and kept beside the sentence; `questions-only` everywhere; outline items `ai-proposed` until edited.
- **AI-ese linter**: Kobak excess-vocabulary words (ratio ≥ 3 or top 100) with the ratio shown; pattern phrases labeled heuristics. **Style-distance meter**: two needles ("to you", "to corpus") per paragraph/section/document over a fixed feature set (median/p90 length, verb position, passive share, we-rate, hedges/intensifiers per 1k, connector openers, inflated and excess vocabulary, type-token ratio, length variance, dash rate); click lists the three sentences pulling furthest from the author; no per-sentence AI score; no detector as a gate. **Language**: paper text English; notes/questions/replies follow `notes_language` (default zh when the author types Chinese); no CJK ever reaches a push.

### Faithfulness and provenance

One pure function `gateSentence(text, ctx)` runs in the ghost path before display, in the PostToolUse hook, and on every rewrite:
1. Numbers: every numeral (unit, %, ±, exponent, `\SI{}`) must equal a fact at its sigfigs or an alias, or be a section/equation/figure/year token, or a declared derived relation (`2.4 times` from two facts via a small calculator), or a number typed in the intent note (linked `unlinked`, amber, visible to Consistency); otherwise `[fact?: <description>]`. Percent-vs-fraction and factor-vs-percent mismatches flagged.
2. Cuts/definitions match `cut`/`definition` facts or become placeholders.
3. Citations outside the resolved set → `[ref]`; `\ref`/`\eqref` targets must exist in the flattened project.
4. LaTeX invariance for rewrites: identical multisets of `\cite`, `\ref`, `\label`, `\eqref` arguments, math spans and macros, else rejected.
5. No number from the precomputed set of the skill's fictional-example numbers.
6. Placeholders descriptive and unique; export and push blocked while any remain.
7. Claim strength (deterministic lexicon): "exclude"/"rule out" needs a limit fact with CL ≥ 95%; "demonstrate"/"establish" need `validation_state ≥ reproduced`; a result verb with no claim id is flagged. Reread hints, never errors.
8. Tier-1 style_check + AI-ese.

Sentences are never silently repaired. Every version row is an event in `history.jsonl` (model id from the API response, prompt hash, instruction, language, before/after hashes, edit distance). `GET /disclosure?venue=aps|elsevier|arxiv|springer` emits an editable `disclosure.tex` with counts (N drafted from outline and result files, M edited with mean edit distance X%, K rendered from the authors' notes, L language-polished; how verified) plus `ai_use_record.json`.

Sentence card (hover 400 ms or Ctrl+.): state/model/time; move and paragraph job; fact chips (`f.g1.delta_points = 2.3 ± 0.3 points` → Facts entry with artifact + SHA); cite keys with titles; the Chinese note; lint flags with rule id and corpus rate; actions.
- Panels: Outline (five editable items with key phrase highlighted wherever it occurs; section tree with jobs, claim chips unsaid/said/dropped, drag to reorder, per-section ghost mode, Draft buttons); Facts (searchable table, where-used, unresolved `[fact?]` list, manual entry always `candidate`); Review queue; Questions (≤ 4 referee questions per section, anchored, in the notes language, never inserted); Consistency; Terminology (feeds `style_check --known`); Figure plan.
- PDF: PDF.js; compile on save (2 s debounce) via `latexmk -pdf -synctex=1 -interaction=nonstopmode -outdir=.paper/build` on the shadow copy; Ctrl+Shift+J forward sync (`synctex view`); PDF click → source line (one sentence); log drawer links errors to lines.
- Overleaf: Phase 1 `paper pull` / `paper push`; Phase 2 Sync panel. Pull = `git pull --rebase` (or `ols`), re-anchor, list "co-authors changed N sentences" as `external` with diffs. Push shows the exact diff and is **refused** while `proposed`/`unsupported` sentences, placeholders or intent lines exist (proposed sentences can be parked); never force, never rename files.

### Architecture

Processes: (1) browser front end (Vite, React, CodeMirror 6, PDF.js); (2) Node backend (TypeScript, Fastify, one WebSocket): project store + SQLite; segmenter; anchor/three-way service; ghost service (direct Messages API via `@anthropic-ai/sdk`); agent service (Agent SDK); literature client with rate limiters + cache; compile service (latexmk, synctex, file watcher); sync service (git, `ols` subprocess); lint orchestrator; disclosure generator; (3) Python sidecar `lintd.py`: long-lived JSON-lines over stdio, imports `style_check` once from the skill path, wraps `style_check.flag` at runtime to attach `Sent.i`, `block`, `raw` without touching the user's file; serves `lint.sentence {text, part, known[]}` (sentence-local checks 1, 2, 4, 5, local parts of 3 and 10), `lint.document {flattened_tex}` (all checks, reconciled to units by line + normalized excerpt), `corpus.stats {texts[]}`; (4) latexmk + synctex.

APIs: WebSocket `doc.changes` (CodeMirror change sets; backend mirror), `sentences.updated`, `ghost.request|result|cancel`, `sentence.action`, `plan.updated`, `lint.update`, `compile.status`, `sync.status`, `job.progress`; REST `POST /ghost`, `POST /sentence/:id/{instruct|alternatives|grammar|accept|reject}`, `GET /sentence/:id/history`, `POST /agents/{read-repo|draft|review}` (SSE jobs), `GET /synctex/{forward|inverse}`, `POST /sync/{pull|push}`, `GET /style/distance`, `POST /facts/rescan`, `PATCH /facts/:id`, `POST /lit/resolve`, `GET|PUT /outline`, `GET /disclosure`.

## Implementation phases

**Phase 0, spikes (≈ 2 weeks).** Deliverables: repo scaffold + CI; `scripts/bench/ttft.ts` (Haiku 5.5 thinking-off/low vs Sonnet 5.5 between_tools; 10k/27k/50k cached prefix; cold/warm; 50 runs; p50/p95 TTFT and total for a 40-token structured sentence); `prewarm-format.ts` (does `max_tokens: 0` accept `output_config.format`; read `cache_read_input_tokens`); skill-discovery test of the `cwd` + `settingSources: ['project']` + symlink layout (init message must list `hep-paper-writing`); `packages/core` segmenter (unified-latex positions + mask math/`\cite`/`\ref`/`\label` + the skill's `ABBR` and next-char rule) compared with `style_check.py --dump` on the fixture paper; `lintd.py` linting 1,000 sentences; a CodeMirror ghost widget prototype under Chinese IME composition (suppress between `compositionstart`/`compositionend`); deterministic extractor on Canto `results/`; a 20-sentence blind Haiku-vs-Sonnet preference test by the author. Acceptance: one script prints the latency table; ≥ 98% sentence-boundary agreement with `style_check --dump`; pinyin typed over a visible ghost without glitches; ≥ 50 facts extracted and 10 README numbers traced to artifacts. Decision rule: p50 total < 1.2 s and p95 < 2.0 s keeps the pause trigger in `auto-propose` sections; otherwise boundary-only there and on-demand elsewhere.

**Phase 1a, review core usable on Canto (≈ 4–5 weeks part-time; no ghost yet).** Import (git clone or zip) with optional reversible one-sentence-per-line normalization; editor + compile + PDF sync; sentence units, sidecar, re-anchoring on reload and `paper pull`; repo brief + extractor + Facts panel; five-item form + outline with three framings; **retro-grounding** of the imported draft (every numeral linked to a fact or listed as unmatched); mode B on one section with the gate, structure pass and review queue; sentence card actions (accept, edit, instruct with Chinese notes, grammar-only, rewrite myself, reject → `[claim:…?]`, history); tier-1 lint + AI-ese; provenance log + APS disclosure text; `paper pull` / `paper push` with the push guard. Acceptance (the author runs it): open the Canto clone; click a sentence, type a Chinese instruction, get a rewrite with a Chinese note and intact `\cite`/`\ref`/math, accept it; ask for a sentence with a number absent from the facts and see a placeholder; commit a 45-word sentence and see the long-sentence hint with the corpus rate; revise the Results section end to end with zero unmatched numerals, zero unresolved cite keys and zero `proposed` sentences left; pull an advisor edit and see ≥ 95% of sentence ids preserved with the edited sentence marked `external`; see push refused while one `proposed` sentence remains; export the disclosure paragraph with correct counts.

**Phase 1b, ghost on demand + voice v0 (≈ 4–5 weeks).** `complete-my-sentence` ghost on Ctrl+Space with the cached A/B/C prefix, pre-warm and atomic paint; intent lines with Ctrl+Enter / Alt+Enter rendering and the shadow compile; corpus import from Drive PDFs/Docs (the app asks which texts were unassisted), measured norms, BM25 exemplar retrieval, a hand-written `memo.md`; Questions (lite). Explicit stubs through Phase 1: no memo learning; literature = key resolution only; no auto-ghost; no Sync panel; single `.tex` (no `\input` flattening); Consistency/Terminology/Figure plan absent; every Phase 2 feature behind a flag; the Phase 1a acceptance test re-run weekly.

**Phase 2 (≈ 6–8 weeks).** Auto-ghost per section with keep-alive; the re-planner in both modes with `fulfilled_move_ids`; drafter with resume/fork; lit-reader with `quotable[]` records and the citation gate; CIPHER memo learning and the style meter; Consistency (with stale propagation), Terminology, Figure plan, Questions panels; Sync panel with `ols` fallback; venue templates; derived-fact calculator. Acceptance: an advisor edits in Overleaf and a pull keeps every unchanged id and lists the changed ones; a planted abstract-vs-conclusions drift is caught; a re-run analysis marks a fact stale and its sentences appear in Consistency; after 20 edits the memo updates and the author edits a bullet.

**Phase 3 (≈ 4–6 weeks, as needed).** VS Code client; `\input` flattening; Batch API overnight referee and consistency passes; a Sonnet "does the quote support this sentence" check over `quotable[]`; Tauri packaging; voice shared across papers; FCC-ee and LDMX notes as second documents.

Honest total: 5–6 months part-time for one developer with Claude Code. The CodeMirror sentence/ghost layer plus anchoring is the hard core; the agents are the easy part. Phase 1a alone already pays off on Canto.

## Verification

- **Unit tests** (vitest in `packages/core`): segmenter against the fixture and against `style_check --dump` (≥ 98% boundary agreement; abbreviations `Fig.~\ref`, `Eq.`, `et al.`, inline math, `\cite` kept inside sentences); anchoring (edits, external three-way diffs, splits, orphans); `gateSentence` (numbers/aliases/derived relations, cite allowlist, LaTeX invariance, fictional-number block, claim strength, percent-vs-factor); normalizers and hashes; prompt builders produce byte-identical blocks A/B across requests (cache stability).
- **Sidecar tests** (pytest): `lintd.py` import, per-sentence vs per-document flags, reconciliation to units, 1,000-sentence timing.
- **End-to-end** (Playwright, Chromium preinstalled; `--trace off`): open fixture, segment, click sentence → card, instruct → diff → accept; proposed-state review queue; push guard refusal; ghost widget under simulated IME composition.
- **Benchmarks**: `scripts/bench/ttft.ts` and `prewarm-format.ts` run on the laptop with the API key (api.anthropic.com may be blocked in this sandbox); results recorded in `docs/adr/`.
- **Compile**: no TeX in the container; compile/SyncTeX tests run on the laptop (or in a later session after installing TeX Live); CI skips them when `latexmk` is absent.
- **Acceptance**: the Phase 1a checklist above, run by the author on the real Canto clone; weekly re-run.

## Risks and open questions

1. Ghost latency above 1.2 s feels broken → Phase 0 measures; on-demand default outside `auto-propose`; atomic paint; Sonnet promotable by the blind test.
2. Haiku at low effort may leak reasoning text or write flat English → structured output or stop sequence, the gate, one regeneration, the blind test.
3. Chinese IME with a widget at the cursor → suppress ghost during composition; block widget below the line as fallback; test Chrome and Safari in Phase 0.
4. Co-author edits break anchors or reflow lines → semantic line breaks, fuzzy alignment, orphan list, no automatic metadata deletion. Question for the author: will Frankenthal accept one-sentence-per-line `.tex`? (Normalization stays optional and reversible.)
5. Facts stale or ambiguous (README vs `results/`) → locator-only extraction, fingerprint staleness, default `candidate`, prose-only numbers flagged; the app asks which files are authoritative per repo.
6. Voice corpus small or already AI-touched → the import step asks which texts were unassisted; lean on measured norms and the memo; show the meter with corpus size.
7. The outline anchors thinking → author-first form, three framings, outline provenance, `questions-only` mode.
8. Scope, data and licensing: one developer; unpublished results leave the laptop through the API (local-first, key in keychain, `.paperignore`, Anthropic data policy documented); MIT/Apache dependencies only; frozen stub list and weekly acceptance.
